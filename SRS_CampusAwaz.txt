2. INTRODUCTION

2.1 Purpose

CampusAwaz is an AI-powered, privacy-first student complaint and support platform
designed to connect verified university students and eligible alumni with university
administrations. This SRS defines the functional, non-functional, UI/UX, integration,
architecture, security, notification, analytics, and acceptance requirements for the
CampusAwaz MVP.

2.2 Scope

The system will allow students from participating universities to verify their university
affiliation, submit university-related complaints, choose their preferred level of identity
protection, upload evidence, receive AI assistance, track complaints, and receive
resolution information.

University authorities will be able to manage, assign, review, escalate, and resolve
complaints through role-based dashboards. Sensitive harassment and safety complaints
will use a protected workflow with restricted access.

The MVP will focus on a responsive web application and a complete complaint-to-
resolution workflow.

22. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
CampusAwaz will be considered acceptable for the MVP when:

    1. A student can register and select a participating university.
    2. A student can verify their university affiliation through the supported verification

         flow.
    3. A verified student can submit a university-related complaint.
    4. The student can choose Identified, Confidential, or Anonymous reporting.
    5. The student can attach supporting evidence.
    6. The AI Complaint Assistant can analyze a complaint and provide applicable

         recommendations.
    7. AI-generated complaint information can be reviewed before submission.
    8. Harassment and safety complaints use a protected workflow.
    9. Sensitive cases are restricted to authorized personnel.
    10. Every submitted complaint receives a tracking ID.
    11. Students can view complaint progress through the defined status timeline.
    12. Administrators can view and manage complaints according to their roles.
    13. Administrators can assign and update complaints.
    14. Complaints can enter the escalation workflow when the defined response period is

         exceeded.
    15. Administrators can provide resolution information and Proof of Action.
    16. Students can view the resolution information.
    17. Relevant complaint status changes can generate notifications.
    18. The system provides the specified administration analytics.
    19. The application works across desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
    20. The complete core journey works from verification through resolution:
Verify  Protect  Report  AI Understands  Route  Track  Escalate  Prove  Resolve
